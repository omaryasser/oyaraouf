---
title: "Time Is Limited - Do Not Learn Every New Thing"
date: "2025-09-20"
type: "Post"
---

Time is limited. Don’t waste it in learning, trying, and switching to every new fancy tool out there.

Whether it’s note-taking, task management, programming languages, diagramming, code editors, or backend/frontend frameworks, most tools will get the job done. There’s a scale threshold after which the decision matters, but in most cases, especially at the individual level, almost any option will work.

When I hear about a new tool, I spend about 10 minutes understanding its edge, power, or differentiator, just enough to know what’s out there in the market. But I rarely see a paradigm shift, so I rarely switch. For example, I’ve been using the same note-taking app and diagramming tools for the last five years. I dive into the details only as much as I need to get the job done.

Look at the bigger picture:
* Spend time learning and strengthening your engineering foundations.
* Spend time on transferable skills.
* Spend time understanding the business side.
* Spend time building tools that serve your community.

Specializing is good, but you won’t be able to specialize in everything.
